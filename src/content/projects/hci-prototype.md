An HCI team prototype in React Native and Expo, with implementation contributions credited to Fabius Grünhagen.

One dashboard lists pending and completed habits; the other focuses on the next action. Both support confirmation, undo, streak feedback, and confetti when the day’s habits are done. Shared sample data keeps the comparison about interaction design.
